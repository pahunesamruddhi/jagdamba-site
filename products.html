One range, 10 to 1,000 tons.

02–08 · The rest of the system

Around every press, a full fluid-power catalogue.

Cylinders to move it, power packs to drive it, manifolds to control it, lifts and gates that put it to work — and the components that keep all of it sealed and running.

02 Hydraulic & pneumatic cylinders
Mill duty, heavy duty, tie-rod, telescopic and fully custom — for mobile and industrial applications, OEM and end user. PTFE low-friction seals, bronze guide tapes, long service life. Steel, cement, chemical & power plants; wagon tipplers, side-arm chargers, stacker reclaimers; earth-moving and loader cylinders. OEM + custom
import substitute 03 Hydraulic power packs
Low-noise power units for lifting platforms, elevators, wagon tipplers, presses, machine tools, mobile and flameproof (FLP) applications — internationally reputed components, sized to your duty cycle. AC and DC packs, hand pumps, jacks, tippler power packs — cost-effective without compromising quality. Lifts · tipplers · presses
built to duty cycle 04 Manifold blocks
From the smallest unit to large system manifolds. We design, develop and manufacture to your requirement — and replace existing OEM manifolds one-for-one with quality-matched blocks. Standard range: lift block, PCM block, regeneration block, sequence block, 1–4 station blocks. Lift · PCM · sequence
1–4 station 05 Hydraulic cylinder kits
Our newest line: mill-duty, welded-construction and heavy-duty kits. Each kit complete with back end, gland (head) and pistons — supplied with or without seals. Top-grade kits for builders and repairers who machine their own tubes. New line
ready stock 06 Goods, cargo & passenger lifts
Hydraulic goods and cargo lifts engineered for factories and warehouses, plus hydraulic passenger lift systems — smooth, compact machine-room-friendly drives. Complete with cylinder, power pack, manifold and controls from one workshop. Factory & warehouse
single-source supply 07 Hydraulic gates & actuators
Bunker-mouth and industrial gates with fail-safe automatic closure on power failure, remote electric control, CCTV + PA monitoring and a dust-proof AC operator cabin — built around our own cylinders and packs. Fail-safe · CCTV & PA · remote panel · heavy-duty wear-resistant build. Industrial & irrigation
turnkey 08 Components & consumables
Hydraulic accessories and seals: V, U and Vdas seals, O-rings, wipers, Sharron seal kits, guide tape, bronze-filled PTFE. Plus honed tubes and hard-chrome plated rods, cut to size. Valves and pumps of reputed makes (Polyhydron, Yuken, Rexroth class), DCVs, check valves, gear pumps, filters, gauges, hoses and fittings. Seals · tubes · rods
