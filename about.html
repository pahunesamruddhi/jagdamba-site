About

A technocrat company,
built on twenty years of oil and steel.

Shree Jagdamba Hydropneumatic — trademark JAGDAMBA — designs, develops and manufactures hydraulic cylinders and fluid-power equipment from a state-of-the-art facility at MIDC Butibori, Nagpur: the geographic centre of India.

The work

Import substitutes, Indian lead times.

Our specialty is developing import-substitute products: cylinders, manifolds and complete machines built to OEM specification, delivered at Indian lead times and Indian costs. We serve a very wide spectrum of applications — and we are continuously working to produce reliable products with the best quality. Customer satisfaction is the aim; it has been since 2006.

Principle 01

Quality control, end to end

A documented quality system from plate to pressure test — backed by a 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Principle 02

Unrivalled workmanship

Professional, qualified engineers and fitters; machining done after normalising for true parallelism and squareness.

Principle 03

Environmental sensitivity

Leak-free manifold drives, low-noise packs and long-life components — machines that waste less by design.

Principle 04

Personalised solutions

Every machine specified to your component, cycle time and shop floor. Consultancy included, jargon optional.

The works

Made here. Tested here.

A working shop, not a showroom: presses, cylinders, manifolds and power packs built and tested on-site at MIDC Butibori.

Track record

From one bay in Butibori
to a pan-India installed base.

Our machines run in utensil plants, auto-component shops, FRP and rubber units, and in steel, cement, chemical and power plants across the country — alongside equipment-manufacturer clients who build on our cylinders and manifolds. Certified, and proudly Make in India.

2006

Founded at MIDC Butibori

Nagpur, Maharashtra

Shree Jagdamba Hydropneumatic opens as a hydraulic cylinder and repair workshop in central India.

2010s

Full fluid-power range

Presses · power packs · manifolds

The catalogue grows to complete machines — deep-draw presses to 1,000 tons (CNC servo), power packs, manifold systems, lifts and gates.

2021–24

On the international stage

Raipur · HIMTEX · IESS (EEPC India) · IMS

From the 2021 Steel · Power · Cement · Mining Expo in Raipur onwards, we exhibit at HIMTEX Hyderabad, IMS Bangalore and EEPC India's International Engineering Sourcing Show, demonstrating presses to overseas delegations in Coimbatore and Chennai.

2024

425 T servo press

Central India's biggest servo deep-draw press

Servo-hydraulic drives join the range, with lower energy use, near-silent running and saved programs. A hydropower gate project is commissioned the same year.

2024

Cylinder kits launched

Newest product line

Mill-duty, welded and heavy-duty kits — back end, gland and pistons, with or without seals.

2026

Connected & automated

IoT control · robotic forming · 600 T

IoT-enabled remote operation, robot-fed deep draw lines and 600-ton presses. The servo range is shown at HIMTEX 2026.

Today

20 years of engineered force

30+ machine types · pan-India

Two decades in, the aim is unchanged: reliable products, best quality, satisfied customers.
