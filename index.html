Who we are

A veteran workshop with an engineer's stubbornness.

Since 2006 we have done one thing: fluid power, end to end. We design the frame on FEA software, fabricate it from ultrasonically tested steel, machine it after normalising, build the hydraulics around a compact manifold — and then stand behind the machine for its working life.

Much of our work replaces imported equipment one-for-one: cylinders, manifolds and presses built to OEM specification, at Indian lead times and Indian costs.

How we build

Designed to outlast its operator's career.

Every Jagdamba press shares one drive philosophy: a compact manifold block instead of piping, internationally reputed valves, and our own cylinders. Fewer joints, fewer leaks, constant pressure through the cycle.

01 — Frame

FEA-designed, ultrasonically tested steel

Frames cut from prime steel plates, designed on FEA software to resist deflection, with vibration-damping capability built in.

02 — Guiding

Precision slide guidance

Extra-long, machined and hardened guides with centralised lubrication deliver lasting accuracy, alignment and reduced wear.

03 — Hydraulics

Compact manifold drive

The Jagdamba system replaces piping with a single block. In-house cylinders run PTFE low-friction seals on bronze guide tapes.

04 — Controls

Operator-safe control panel

Manual, semi and full-auto modes with safety interlocks. Proximity sensors on the top cylinder set the exact stroke required.
