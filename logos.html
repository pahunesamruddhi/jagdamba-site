Identity exploration · Jagdamba

Three marks for a veteran.

Each direction is built from the brand strategy — engineered force, twenty years, import-substitute confidence — in the Heavy Industry Modern language: geometric construction, ink + signal orange, nothing decorative. All three share the same wordmark typography (Inter SemiBold, tight tracking, sentence-feel) so the site's existing identity evolves rather than resets.

Direction A

The Pressmark

The J becomes the machine: a square frame (the press body), the letter's stem as the descending ram, an orange cylinder cap driving it. Literal enough for a fabricator to read instantly, abstract enough to last thirty years. Strongest as an all-purpose industrial mark — stencils, machine plates, embroidery.

Direction B

The Tonnage Seal

A certification stamp — the visual language of inspection plates and pressure-test certificates. "JAGDAMBA · HYDROPNEUMATIC" rings a bold J, with EST 2006 carried like a hallmark. This is the most "veteran" of the three: it borrows authority from the marks engineers already trust. Excellent as a quality seal beside the wordmark, on test reports, packaging and the machines themselves.

Direction C

The Daylight Wordmark

Pure typography under load. The wordmark sits on a heavy ink baseline — the press bed — while an orange platen bar hovers above it. The gap between bar and letters is the "daylight" of a press: the working space where force is applied. Quietly clever, extremely versatile, and the closest evolution of the site's current identity. Recommendation

System, not single mark: adopt A (Pressmark) as the primary mark + wordmark for everyday use, B (Tonnage Seal) as the certification device on machines, test reports and expo material, and keep C's daylight bars as a typographic motif for headings and document covers. That's exactly how veteran industrial houses behave — one workhorse mark, one seal of authority, one typographic signature.

Colours: ink #191B1F, signal orange #E8521D, paper #FDFDFC; single-colour versions reproduce in ink only.
