## Interaction philosophy (the "flat after hero" fix)

- index/engineering → hover-linked press schematic (locates each claim on the machine)
- products → selectable model rows feeding the quote flow
- calculators → already the most functional page on the site
- expos → photo placeholders structured for real stand imagery
Everything else: calm.
