How a repair runs

Four steps. No surprises.

Every job follows the same documented sequence, whether it's a 50 mm pneumatic cylinder or a 400-ton press overhaul.

Step 1

Strip & inspect

Full tear-down, dimensional report and photographs of every wear surface — you approve the scope before we touch a tool.

Step 2

Hone & replace

Bores honed in-house; rods re-plated or replaced from our hard-chrome stock; damaged glands and pistons re-machined.

Step 3

Reseal

PTFE low-friction seal kits and bronze guide tapes — the same specification we fit on new Jagdamba cylinders.

Step 4

Test & certify

Pressure-tested above working pressure, cycled, and dispatched with a test report.
